🗳️ Elections in India

Everything you need to know about the world's largest democracy

01
Announcement
Election Commission announces election schedule, Model Code of Conduct begins.
02
Nominations
Candidates file nomination papers with returning officer. Scrutiny and withdrawal periods follow.
03
Campaigning
Parties and candidates campaign under Model Code of Conduct. Expenditure limits apply.
04
Voting Day
Voters cast votes using EVMs (Electronic Voting Machines) with VVPAT verification slips.
05
Counting
Votes counted at counting centers under strict observation. Results declared constituency-wise.
06
Government Formation
Winning party/alliance with majority forms government. CM/PM sworn in by Governor/President.
